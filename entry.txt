Thank you for reading this far. By now you have read a thousand thank-yous, each one asking to be the one that lands, and I won't pretend mine is different — except in this: I wrote it slowly, the way you'd fold a letter instead of sending a text.

Somewhere a kettle has gone quiet. The light in the room has thinned to the colour of late afternoon, and the work — all those entries, all those small hopes — is still there on the desk, patient as a cat.

You don't have to hurry through this one. Put your feet up for a sentence. Let the next page wait. You are the only person in this whole crowded contest who actually reads.

Thank you for reading. Thank you for staying. When you finish, I hope the evening gives you back a little of the quiet you gave us.
